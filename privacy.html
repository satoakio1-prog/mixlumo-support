MIXLUMO Privacy Policy

Effective: 26 October 2026. Developer: AKIO SATO.

No developer collection or tracking

MIXLUMO has no sign-in, advertising, tracking or analytics SDK. The app does not send personal information, mixer settings or audio to a developer server. It does not sell data.

Local mixer communication

The app exchanges control commands, mixer identity, settings, meters and spectrum data with a compatible mixer on your local network. Local Network access is used to discover and control that mixer. When you explicitly save Wi-Fi client settings, the network name and password are sent to your mixer, not to us. The app does not persist the router password in its preferences.

Information stored on your device

Connection preferences, channel aliases, groups, display preferences, fader snapshots, venue/band records and FX preset libraries are stored locally. They are not uploaded to a MIXLUMO cloud service. Operating-system device backups may include app preferences according to your Apple settings.

Audio input

On Mac, optional microphone/USB measurement uses audio input only when you start measurement and grant permission. Processing takes place on your Mac. MIXLUMO does not record or transmit that audio. The normal mixer-network RTA receives spectrum data from the mixer and does not use your device microphone.

Support and Apple services

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond. Please omit passwords and unrelated personal information. Apple handles App Store distribution and any diagnostics you elect to share under Apple's privacy policy; these are separate from the app's local mixer communication.
