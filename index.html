MIXLUMO

A touch mixer controller for Behringer XR18 and X18, with Mac, iPhone and iPad layouts.

A compatible physical mixer is required for live control. Connect the Apple device and mixer to the same local network. Wi-Fi or Ethernet carries control commands and meter data, not multichannel audio. Audio playback/recording through the mixer requires a separate supported USB audio connection and audio software.

Getting connected

  1. Choose the mixer hardware mode: ACCESS POINT, Wi-Fi CLIENT or ETHERNET.
  2. Join the matching network on your Apple device. Allow Local Network access when requested.
  3. Open CONNECT and use FIND & CONNECT. Controls become available after values arrive from the identified mixer.

If your router password changed, first connect directly through ACCESS POINT or Ethernet. Open the client setup, select or enter the 2.4 GHz network name and enter the new password. After saving, switch the mixer to Wi-Fi CLIENT and join the same router.

Working with the mixer

Select a channel for its input, EQ/RTA, dynamics and sends. FX editing controls the mixer's hardware DSP. SOUND ASSIST contains operator-started setup tools; inspect and listen to their results before a show. Feedback detection can also detect sustained musical tones and is not a guarantee against feedback. Fader snapshots store fader levels, not complete mixer scenes. Dual Pitch editing is unavailable.
